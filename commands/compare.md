---
description: Compare two or more UI styles and recommend one for a stated constraint
argument-hint: [a vs b, e.g. glassmorphism vs neumorphism]
---

Use the `ui-design` skill. Compare: `$ARGUMENTS`.

If the constraint is not in the request, ask with `AskUserQuestion` first: audience and sector, accessibility target, platform. A comparison with no constraint is a feature list, and the whole point is the call.

1. **A decision-first table**: rows are the things that decide (contrast behaviour, affordance, density, build cost, platform support, register), columns are the styles. Not a feature matrix.
2. **Recommend one** and say what the others lose.
3. **Name the case that would flip it.** A recommendation nobody can argue with was not a real comparison.
4. **Where they differ on accessibility, say so plainly**, with the ratio or the setting, not as a general caution.

Never conclude "it depends" and stop. Pick, then name the condition under which you would pick differently.
